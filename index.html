Why This System

Built for Water-Smart Sugarcane Farming

Sugarcane is a water-intensive crop. Manual, calendar-based irrigation often leads to over- or under-watering. Our system replaces guesswork with data.

Reduce Water Waste

Irrigate only when the soil and weather data indicate real need, cutting water usage by up to 30%.

Real-Time Sensing

Soil moisture, temperature, and humidity sensors stream field data continuously to the cloud.

AI-Driven Advisory

A trained neural network interprets sensor data and crop growth stage to recommend precise irrigation actions.

Higher Yield

Consistent, optimal soil moisture through each growth stage supports healthier cane and better sucrose content.

How It Works

From Field Sensor to Farmer Decision

Sensor nodes installed in the field measure soil moisture, temperature, and humidity at regular intervals. This data is transmitted wirelessly to a central system, where an AI advisory engine compares it against crop-stage-specific thresholds and weather forecasts.


The system then generates an actionable recommendation — irrigate now, hold, or delay — along with an estimated water quantity, viewable on the dashboard or sent as an alert to the farmer.
